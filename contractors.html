One call for the IT, network, and low voltage side of your project.

What we handle on your project

  • Low voltage and data scope planning — drop counts, rack/IDF locations, pathways, and rough-in coordination with your trades
  • Structured cabling, security cameras, access control, and AV — regulated installation performed by our Oregon CCB-licensed partner contractors
  • Network design, equipment configuration, Wi-Fi planning, and commissioning — our own work
  • Cybersecurity setup, backups, and ongoing managed IT after turnover
  • Test results, labeling, and as-built documentation for closeout

Why GCs work with us

You get a single point of contact who speaks both construction and IT. We show up at rough-in, we coordinate with your electrician and framers, and we turn over a working network, not just cable in the walls. Your tenants or owners can stay with us for IT support after move-in.

How it works

  1. Send plans or a scope — floor plans, spec sections (27/28), or just a site address and a description
  2. Walk-through & written proposal — clear line items, no surprise add-ons
  3. Rough-in & trim — scheduled around your trades
  4. Commissioning & handoff — tested, labeled, documented

Service area

Salem, Keizer, Silverton, Woodburn, Dallas, and the greater Willamette Valley.
