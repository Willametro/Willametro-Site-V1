Marketing for Contractors & Trades

Local SEO, Google ads, and a website that turns searches into phone calls. Built for builders, remodelers, electricians, plumbers, roofers, and HVAC shops in the Willamette Valley.

The problem we solve

Most trades get work from referrals, and that is great until the calendar has a gap. When a homeowner in Salem searches “roofer near me” or “kitchen remodel Keizer,” the shops that show up are the ones with a complete Google Business Profile, steady reviews, a fast website, and clear service pages. We set that up, keep it running, and show you each month which calls came from where.

What’s included

  • Google Business Profile — categories, service areas, photos, posts, Q&A, and a simple review-request routine for your crew
  • Local SEO — service and city pages, consistent name/address/phone across directories (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Houzz), and your CCB number shown where customers check it
  • Website — a fast, mobile-first site with click-to-call, quote forms, project galleries, and the hosting, security, and backups handled by us
  • Paid ads — Google Local Services Ads (the “Google Guaranteed” badge), Google Search ads, and Facebook/Instagram ads, with negative keywords so you stop paying for job seekers and DIYers
  • Tracking & reporting — call tracking and form tracking so every lead is counted, and a one-page monthly report in plain English

How it works

  1. Free review — we look at your Google profile, website, reviews, and top competitors and tell you what we would fix first
  2. Written proposal — a fixed setup price and monthly price, with no surprise add-ons
  3. Setup (2–4 weeks) — profile, listings, site, and tracking go live; you send photos of past jobs and we do the rest
